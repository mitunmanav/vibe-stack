# A fixed limit on questions per round

`/ask-me` asks every question that is ready to be asked (the frontier) in one round. Requests to cap a round, or a whole interview, at a fixed number are out of scope.

## Why this is out of scope

A cap is wrong both ways: it hides questions a small idea could settle at once, and it drags a big idea across many tiny rounds.

It also blurs two different failures. Many questions because the idea is still vague is the interview working. Many questions the user cannot answer means the agent is asking technical things it should have decided itself, and the fix for that is the sorting rule in `clarifying`, not a counter.

The user is already in control: they can answer only the questions they care about, say "use your recommendations", or stop at any time. Plain words are the steering wheel, not a number.
