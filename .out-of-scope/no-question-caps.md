# Out of Scope: A Fixed Limit on Questions per Round

`/ask-me` asks every question that is ready to be asked (the frontier) in one round. It does not cap a round at a fixed number.

## Why this is out of scope

A cap is wrong both ways: it hides questions a small idea could settle at once, and it drags a big idea across many tiny rounds. Two different failures also look alike under a cap. Many questions because the idea is still vague is the interview working. Many questions the user cannot answer means the agent is asking technical things it should have decided itself, and the fix for that is the sorting rule in `clarifying`, not a counter.

The user stays in control: they can answer only the questions they care about, say "use your recommendations", or stop at any time.
